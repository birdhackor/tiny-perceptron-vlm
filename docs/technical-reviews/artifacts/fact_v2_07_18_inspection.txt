Arithmetic and format derivation
1 + 2 = 3. The literal string "3" supplies that value and only decimal digits.
"答案是3喔！" states the same arithmetic result but contains explanatory text,
so it violates the instruction to respond only with a number. 4 != 3, so an
accuracy-only rule that gives 1 to the correct result and 0 to other results
would give the literal sample "4" reward 0. The dictionary's reward field is
manually assigned: the snippet does not implement, sample, or train a scorer.
Changing the task to "explain 1+2 in one sentence" makes explanation part of
the criterion. Merely exchanging the two original preference labels supplies
no explanation; both old strings omit how one and two combine into three.

PPO, arXiv:1707.06347v1, sections 2-3 and 5, equations (1), (7), (9), and

old-policy sampling stage and multiple minibatch updates. The old policy in

continuous control and Atari; its name alone cannot make a signal RLHF.

DeepSeek-R1, arXiv:2501.12948v1 (22 Jan 2025), sections 2.1-2.3:
R1-Zero applies GRPO directly to DeepSeek-V3-Base without preliminary SFT;
2.2.2 uses rule-based accuracy and format rewards, no neural outcome/process RM.
2.3.1 gathers thousands of long-CoT examples, including generated and human
postprocessed examples, to fine-tune V3-Base before reasoning RL. Cold-start data
improves initial readability; it is not synonymous with all-human annotation.
2.3.2 adds language-consistency reward; 2.3.3 later collects ~600k reasoning
and ~200k other examples and runs two SFT epochs on the base; 2.3.4 adds RL
for general scenarios. Therefore the lesson's two routes are valid summaries,
not a claim that the R1 cold-start stage is its complete training pipeline.
R1 uses GRPO; the section accurately says RL rather than claiming R1 uses PPO.

- **Bengio, Vinyals, Jaitly and Shazeer, Scheduled Sampling for Sequence Prediction with Recurrent Neural Networks**, arXiv:1506.03099v3, 23 September 2015. PDF pp. 1–3, abstract and §§1, 2.1–2.4, especially Eq. (1) and §2.3.

Thus reporting 3 as known has no visible justification; this says nothing about its chance coincidence with the hidden truth. 2. Operational criteria: useful, honest, and safe are the lesson's stated goals, evidence constraint, and task boundaries. Safe refusal can omit useful next steps; a long answer can include unsupported claims; assisting one user can violate a privacy boundary.

## Original authorities personally read

Original retrieval/version/SHA receipts are in `original-retrieval-receipts.json`. Complete PDFs/HTML/model cards remain in ignored research; these notes contain my own analysis only.

- **Attention Is All You Need**, arXiv `1706.03762v7`, §3, §3.1, §3.2 and Eq. (1), §3.2.3, §3.3 Eq. (2), §3.4. Attention is a weighted combination of value vectors; the architecture combines learned embeddings, attention, position-wise FFN, residuals and normalization, and the autoregressive decoder uses causal masking and offset targets. This supports R.2's decomposition into understandable components. The original paper is an encoder-decoder with post-norm; this project's initial model is decoder-only and pre-norm. R.2 calls Transformer a combination of components and does not claim that its TinyLM is the literal 2017 architecture.
- **Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback**, arXiv `2203.02155v1`, abstract/introduction and §3.1 (printed p.6). Its next-token objective differs from useful instruction following; its demonstrated recipe starts with a pretrained GPT-3 model, uses human demonstrations for supervised policy training, human comparisons to train an RM, then PPO to optimize the policy. Supports the staged-training and signal distinction route, not a universal necessity of that recipe or guaranteed instruction correctness.
- **Direct Preference Optimization**, arXiv `2305.18290v3`, §3 Eqs. (1)–(3), §4 Eqs. (4)–(7). The Bradley–Terry comparison depends on reward difference; typical RLHF learns an explicit reward and uses RL, commonly PPO. DPO reparameterizes the preference objective in policy/reference probabilities, without an explicit standalone reward-model fit or RL loop. R.2's 13.17 is a branch comparison, not a mandatory PPO→DPO chain. Current `posttraining.py` explicitly implements finite response cards; the linked examples and text distinguish this small experiment from generated-text RLHF and distinguish programmed labels from human feedback.
- **Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct**, official upstream model card and config pinned to `89644892e4d85e24eaac8bacfd4f463576704203`: model identity, image/text message structure and `Qwen3VLForConditionalGeneration`/`AutoProcessor` quickstart. Config names the text and vision parts. Supports the natural branch's mature image/text starting point. I did not adopt model-card marketing superlatives or its benchmark performance as evidence of this course's ability.
- **Whisper-large-v3-turbo**, official `openai` upstream model card pinned to `41f01f3fe87f28c78e2fbf8b568835947dd65ed9`: introduction, Usage transcription versus translation, Model details, Performance and Limitations.

For a fixed context and sampling policy, minimizing E[(v−R)^2] gives derivative 2(v−E[R]) and minimizer v=E[R]. A single target R=1 does not establish that expectation. For the one-element fence, the mean denominator is N=1: L=(v−1)^2, dL/dv=2(v−1). At v=.4 these are .36 and −1.2; at v=1.4 they are .16 and +.8. With learning rate η>0, v_new=v−η dL/dv moves toward this sample target. Both source fences call backward and print gradients; neither calls an optimizer or assigns an updated value.

The first dimension of exact_kl is context and the last is candidate. KL(p||q)=sum_i p_i(ln p_i−ln q_i); the sum is over all candidates, yielding one KL per context. Natural logs give nats. .8 ln(1.6)+.2 ln(.4)=.19274475702175753, rounded .1927. Identical distributions give zero. For normalized positive p, softmax(log p)=p. The implementation detach()s reference logits, uses log_softmax on both and sums the last dimension. It rejects mismatched shapes and non-2D input. These are finite candidate probabilities, not token or sequence likelihoods.

406: 4     Straight-Through Estimator
407: 
408: Another estimator of the expected gradient through stochastic neurons was proposed by Hinton
409: (2012) in his lecture 15b. The idea is simply to back-propagate through the hard threshold function
410: (1 if the argument is positive, 0 otherwise) as if it had been the identity function. It is clearly a biased
411: estimator, but when considering a single layer of neurons, it has the right sign (this is not guaranteed
412: anymore when back-propagating through more hidden layers). We call it the straight-through (ST)
413: estimator. A possible variant investigated here multiplies the gradient on hi by the derivative of the
414: sigmoid. Better results were actually obtained without multiplying by the derivative of the sigmoid.

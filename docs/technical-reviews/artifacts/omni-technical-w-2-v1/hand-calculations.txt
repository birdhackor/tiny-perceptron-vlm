   3<<4=3*16=48=32+16=0b110000.
   5=4+1=0b101. The high and low set bits do not overlap:
   0b110000 OR 0b000101 = 0b110101 = 32+16+4+1=53.

   It illustrates packing for two unsigned four-bit codes, not quantization quality.

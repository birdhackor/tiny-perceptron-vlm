# sft：教學模型

Controlled attribute-extraction TinyLM variants (random-init SFT, text pretraining, and pretrain→SFT), plus a separate 37,728-parameter UltraChat excerpt pilot.

Inspect assistant-only next-token training and compare initialization paths; the natural-text branch demonstrates data preparation and training rather than general conversation quality.

限制：

- The attribute task has only 12 combinations: 9/1/2 train/validation/test families, represented by 45/5/10 task records.
- Exact match and EOS rates are specific to the finite answers and greedy generation; they are not broad instruction-following benchmarks.
- The UltraChat branch uses UTF-8-safe first-turn excerpts capped at 120 bytes each side; fragments may change meaning.
- The reported UltraChat held-out exact match is 0.0 on both validation and test. This pilot is not a competent general chat model.
- pretrain.pt is the completed 250-step pretraining stage, not an assistant SFT model; use non-chat inference for its text continuation.

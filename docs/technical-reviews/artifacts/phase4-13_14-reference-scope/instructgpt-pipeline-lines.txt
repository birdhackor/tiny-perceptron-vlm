300: 3.1       High-level methodology
301: 
302: Our methodology follows that of Ziegler et al. (2019) and Stiennon et al. (2020), who applied
303: it in the stylistic continuation and summarization domains. We start with a pretrained language
304: model (Radford et al., 2019; Brown et al., 2020; Fedus et al., 2021; Rae et al., 2021; Thoppilan et al.,
305: 2022), a distribution of prompts on which we want our model to produce aligned outputs, and a team
306: of trained human labelers (see Sections 3.4 for details). We then apply the following three steps
307: (Figure 2).
308: 
309: Step 1: Collect demonstration data, and train a supervised policy. Our labelers provide demon-
310: strations of the desired behavior on the input prompt distribution (see Section 3.2 for details on this
311: distribution). We then fine-tune a pretrained GPT-3 model on this data using supervised learning.
312:

314: between model outputs, where labelers indicate which output they prefer for a given input. We then

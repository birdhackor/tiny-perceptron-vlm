3.2     Rotary Position Embedding

3.2.1    A 2D case
We start from simple case with dimension d = 2. Under this setting, we make use of the geometric property of vectors
on 2D plane and its complex form to prove (refer to Appendix A for more details) that a solution to our formulation
eq. (11) is:
